# Business notes (context the AI reads before explaining)

- Superstore is a US retailer selling Furniture, Office Supplies and Technology, 2014–2017.
- Customers are businesses and individuals in three segments: Consumer, Corporate, Home Office.
- Main goals: grow sales, keep profit margin healthy, keep customers coming back.
- A profit margin around 10–15% is normal for this kind of retailer. Below 5% is weak; negative means a loss.
- Sales are seasonal: November and December are usually the busiest months; January and February are quiet.
  So compare a month with the same month last year, not only with the month before.
- Discounts are given on many orders. High discounts can turn profitable items into losses.
- Managers act on: pricing, discount limits, regional sales focus, product range, and customer win-back offers.
- Always say an action is a suggestion to check with the business, not a final decision.
